About

Why we keep it small

Ricky
Founder

I started the Grapesyard Project in 2022 as a student at Canadian Academy in Kobe, and it’s now run from the University of Toronto. We keep it small on purpose: one school, one project at a time, and every transfer posted publicly.

The school makes the calls, which is why the blackboards came before the computers. Grapesyard School was founded in 1999 and teaches about 1,400 students from nursery through junior secondary.

If you’d like to see the paperwork behind any line on the ledger, email me and I’ll send it.

FAQ

Common questions

Where does the money go?+

It goes to Grapesyard School for one repair or piece of equipment that the school has chosen and priced. Each transfer is on the ledger with the date, the amount and what it paid for.

Is this a registered charity?+

Not yet. We’re a student-run project that started in 2022 at Canadian Academy in Kobe and is now run from the University of Toronto, so gifts aren’t tax deductible.

Who decides what gets funded?+

The school does. Mr. Edmond tells us what’s needed and in what order, and we raise money for one project at a time.

Can I see proof of a transfer?+

Yes. Email grapesyardproject@gmail.com with the ledger line you want to check, and we’ll send the invoice and the school’s confirmation.

How is this connected to Grapesyard Organization?+

Grapesyard School is run by Grapesyard Organization, a Kenyan nonprofit founded by Mr. Edmond Oloo. The Grapesyard Project is a separate student group that raises money for specific projects at the school.
